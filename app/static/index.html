      New here? How to read this in 3 steps

      1. Pick a place. Click a glowing square on the map to zoom in, choose a big fire event, or draw your own box.
      2. Read the big number. It is how many fire hotspots satellites spotted there in your dates.
      3. Check “vs normal”. 1× is a normal year. Above 1× means more fire than usual, below means less.
      What do the words mean?
      Hotspot
      One small patch of ground a satellite saw burning as it passed over. One big fire makes many hotspots.
      MODIS and VIIRS
      The two satellite fire cameras. MODIS has watched since 2000. VIIRS arrived in 2012 and is sharper, so it counts many more hotspots for the same fires.
      Harmonized
      Our fix: VIIRS counts translated onto the MODIS scale, so 2001 to today is one fair record with no fake jump in 2012.
      Normal
      The middle value (median) for the same dates in past years. Press any “?” button for more help.
